Privacy Policy

Candy Bloom: Sweet Puzzle

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Candy Bloom: Sweet Puzzle (the "App"), published by Afaq Ahamd ("we", "us", "our"). It explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and whether it is shared. We designed the App to respect your privacy: it works fully offline, has no accounts, and does not collect personal data.

Summary: The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any personal information about you. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Account credentials (the App has no sign-in or user accounts)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Advertising IDs or device identifiers
  • Payment or financial information (the App has no real-money purchases)
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage tracking data

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To let you keep playing where you left off, the App saves the following game data locally on your device only (using the device's standard app storage):

DataPurpose
Levels unlocked, stars earned, best scoresSaving your progress
In-game coins and booster counts (Hammer, Shuffle)In-game virtual economy
Daily reward date and streakDaily rewards feature
Missions/achievement progress and statisticsMissions feature
Settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion)Remembering your preferences

This data never leaves your device, is not uploaded to any server, and is not synchronised between devices. It is not linked to your identity. You can erase it at any time via Settings → Reset progress in the App, or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

3. How We Use Information

The locally stored game data is used solely to operate the game features described above. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, because we never receive it.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any user data to any third party, including advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or service providers. Because the App does not transmit data off your device, there are no recipients of your data.

5. Third-Party Services, Ads and SDKs

The current version of the App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The App uses only the standard Flutter framework and a local storage component to save your settings and progress on your device.

If we add advertising or analytics in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy before the change takes effect, describe the types of data collected and the types of third parties involved, and update the data safety information in Google Play accordingly.

6. Permissions

The App does not request any sensitive permissions. Haptic feedback (vibration) and system click sounds are used only for game feedback and can be turned off in Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since the App collects no personal data and contains no ads, social features, or chat, there is no personal information from children to collect, use, or share. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We hold no data about you. Locally stored game data remains on your device until you reset progress in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes all App data from your device.

9. Data Security

Because your game data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us, it is protected by your device's own security features (such as screen lock and app sandboxing). No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device protected.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, or to object to its processing. Since we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on our side. You can remove all locally stored data yourself at any time as described in Section 8.

11. International Users

The App can be used worldwide. Because no data is transmitted to us or stored on our servers, no cross-border transfer of personal data takes place.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at this same URL. Material changes will be reflected in the App's Google Play listing where required. Please review this page periodically.
